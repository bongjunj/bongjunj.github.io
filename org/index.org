I'm interested in applying formal methods (PL, Logics) and AI to
real-world systems. So far, I have focused on compiler research:
automatically finding optimization bugs and optimizations rules
with formal guarantees.

You can find me on [[https://github.com/bongjunj][Github]], or email me via bongjun.jang@kaist.ac.kr.
